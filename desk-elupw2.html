Kapon reminders

  • Valid university ID and the confirmation code (or QR).
  • Dogs and cats only, at least 6 months old and healthy.
  • Up to 2 pets per owner. Limited to 80 pets in total.
  • First come, first served: batch 1 in the morning, batch 2 in the afternoon.
  • The owner is given a consent form to sign for the kapon.
  • Kapon runs in the auditorium while the talks are ongoing.
  • The veterinary team has the final say on every pet.

Anti-rabies reminders

  • Dogs and cats, at least 3 months old and healthy.
  • Up to 2 pets per owner.
  • Ask to see the vaccination card. If the last anti-rabies shot has not expired, vaccinating again is not recommended.
  • The veterinary team has the final say on every pet.
